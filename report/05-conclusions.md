# Conclusions

- **The idea is new in its combination, not in its parts.** Portfolios of ETFs, goal-based investing and inflation-linked bonds are all well studied. No published design I found combines them for an investor who spends in reais.
- **It makes rational sense.** Two blocks protect spending in reais. The third block grows wealth worldwide, with the most risk and the most expected return.
- **It applies the course end to end.** Factor regression and clustering choose the ETFs. Mean-variance optimization with equilibrium inputs sets the weights. Backtests, Monte Carlo and a deflated Sharpe ratio test them.
- **The gap is real.** Almost no one has tested an optimized portfolio of ETFs against one world ETF, after fees. This project fills that gap.
- **The main risk is a tie.** The literature suggests the optimizer may not beat equal weight or one world ETF by the 0.1 Sharpe margin. If it does not, the simple option wins, and that is a valid result.

The open items are short histories, the caps per country and currency, and the Brazilian tax rules. The path to Checkpoint B is in [Next steps](06-next-steps.md).
